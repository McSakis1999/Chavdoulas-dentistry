# Elite Dentistry Launch & SEO Plan

To successfully launch the website and attract patients, follow this comprehensive plan:

## 1. Local SEO & Google Business Profile
*   **Claim your Profile:** Ensure the Google Business Profile is claimed and verified.
*   **Optimization:** Add high-quality photos of the clinic, the team, and results (with consent).
*   **Reviews:** Implement a system to ask happy patients for Google reviews. This is the #1 factor for local rankings.
*   **NAP Consistency:** Ensure Name, Address, and Phone number are identical across the web (website, social media, directories).

## 2. Content Strategy (Organic Traffic)
*   **Blog Section:** Start the blog with 5-10 high-quality articles targeting local keywords (e.g., "Best dentist in [City]", "Invisalign costs [City]").
*   **FAQ Optimization:** The FAQ page is already keyword-rich. Expand it based on actual patient questions.
*   **Service Pages:** Each service has its own section. Ensure they include local keywords and clear benefits.

## 3. Multilingual SEO
*   **Hreflang Tags:** The website already supports English and Spanish. Ensure Google knows which version to show to which users (handled via `react-helmet-async` in the code).
*   **Localized Content:** Don't just translate; localize. Mention specific local landmarks or areas that tourists might recognize.

## 4. Technical SEO
*   **Performance:** The site is built with Vite for fast loading.
*   **Mobile First:** The design is fully responsive, which is critical for Google rankings.
*   **Meta Tags:** Every page has unique titles and descriptions optimized for search.

## 5. Conversion Optimization (CRO)
*   **Clear CTAs:** "Book an Appointment" is prominent on every page.
*   **Trust Signals:** Testimonials and ratings are showcased on the homepage.
*   **Easy Contact:** The contact form is simple and mobile-friendly.

## 6. Launch Checklist
1.  [ ] Verify all links work.
2.  [ ] Test the contact form.
3.  [ ] Submit the sitemap to Google Search Console.
4.  [ ] Set up Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to track traffic.
5.  [ ] Announce the launch on social media and to existing patient lists.
